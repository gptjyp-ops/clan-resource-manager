Register your resources, step by step

First visit? Use your clan invitation. Already have a personal ticket? Upload it on the first screen to enter your clan and sign in.

01
New members: enter your clan

Open the leader’s invitation, enter the clan entry password or access code, and open the clan. This is separate from your personal edit password.

02
Returning members: use your ticket image

Select “Enter with personal QR ticket” and choose the saved image containing its QR code. It opens the linked clan and loads your resources, even after the clan entry password changes.

03
Use your existing game nickname

New members enter their nickname in registration. Existing members without a ticket can sign in with their nickname and edit password. Do not create a second nickname for the same person.

04
Prepare six screenshots

Skills → Egg summons → Egg merges → Mount summons → Mount merges → Green potions. Choose all-resources mode for a complete first registration, or partial-save mode for selected updates.

Uploading or scanning a photo does not save your clan record. Check the numbers, save, then confirm success.

8. Check and save

01
Compare every value

Check amounts, summon levels, current stages and both merge totals. Zero is valid. A scan can misread 111 as 1 without displaying an error.

02
Authenticate your save

First registration requires a personal edit password. Existing password users use their current password. When signed in with a valid QR ticket, you can save without entering a password.

03
Choose the appropriate save mode

All-resources mode requires all four groups. Partial saves preserve blank fields. Entering 0 explicitly changes that amount to zero. Deleting a photo does not erase its amount; save to apply server photo removal.

04
Confirm success

Wait for the success message, then check your nickname and amounts in Clan overview. A failed connection or update notice is not proof of a successful save.

9. Issue and keep your personal ticket

01
Get your ticket

Existing members sign in with their current nickname and edit password, then use the ticket issuance feature. New members complete registration first and follow the ticket prompt.

02
Save the ticket image

Select Save ticket image. On phones you may need to hold the opened image and save it to Photos. Confirm the file exists before selecting the acknowledgement button.

03
Return using the saved image

On the first screen select Enter with personal QR ticket and choose the image containing its QR. The decorative lottery numbers alone cannot sign you in. The image can also be transferred to another device.

04
Keep a current copy

A stored ticket supports personal sign-in when the clan screen opens. Clearing browser data, private mode or another in-app browser may require importing it again. Reissuing a ticket or recovering the password invalidates old tickets; save the new image.

Your ticket grants access to edit your resources. Never post it in group chats. Removed members cannot sign in with an old ticket.

10. Weekly overview and calculators

Weekly registration

The week starts on Sunday at 09:00 Korea time. A member who saved at least one amount this week counts, including partial updates. Weekly registered count is different from the total membership. Register current resources again each week.

Name colorStatus
RedNo registration this week
YellowMerge counts or some resources missing
TealComplete

Use missing-registration and missing-merge sorts, or sort each amount and both merge totals ascending/descending. Merge total means selected + extra.

Open only the calculator you need

My resources prefills your amounts; Clan overview prefills clan totals. Change costs, acquisition multiplier and points to match the current event.

11. Photos, scan problems and recovery

Uploading and replacing photos

Select the matching photo area and choose a JPG, PNG or WEBP file up to 10MB. On PC select the correct area and press Ctrl+V or use Paste copied screenshot. If unavailable, save as PNG and select the file. Use photo deletion to replace an old photo, then save to apply the change.

Standard and Compatibility scanning

Start with Standard. If scanning fails or the number differs, try Compatibility using the same photo. Practice without saving is also available. Check decimals, k, zero stages and merge totals even when no issue is shown.

Reporting problems

Scan issues are eligible for automatic error reporting. If delivery fails, do not assume it was received. For a wrong number without an error, tell the operator the expected value and actual result. Never send tickets or passwords.

Lost password or ticket

Use your saved personal recovery code, or ask the clan leader/operator for recovery. Operator one-time codes expire after one use or 10 minutes. Issue and save a new ticket after recovery.

Clan leaders

Use Clan admin to create a clan and manage server and entry information. Keep admin passwords and recovery details separate from invitations. Ordinary members do not need to edit or deploy Google Apps Script.
